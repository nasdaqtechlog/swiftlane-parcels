Frequently asked questions

How do I track my parcel?

Type your tracking number in the box at the top of the page, or click the link in your confirmation email.

How is shipment pricing determined?

Each shipment is reviewed individually. An administrator enters the final manual costing and tracking code before confirming the booking.

How and when do I pay?

We send an invoice by email. Payment is by bank transfer or cash before dispatch unless you have a business account.

What items can't you carry?

See the prohibited items list in our terms page. This includes weapons, drugs, flammable goods and loose batteries.

What if my parcel is late or damaged?

Contact our support team with your tracking number. Add your claims process and response times to the terms before launch.

Do you help with customs for international parcels?

Yes. We tell you which documents are needed and help you complete them.
