Frequently asked questions

How do I track a parcel?

Enter the tracking number supplied by SwiftLane in the tracking box at the top of this page. The tracking page shows the latest available shipment updates.

What details do I need for a quote?

Share the pickup and delivery locations, parcel type, weight, dimensions and preferred pickup date. Our team will confirm the service and final price.

Can I request a pickup?

Yes. Choose door pickup in the quote form and include your full pickup address and preferred time window. Availability must be confirmed by our team.

Do you ship internationally?

We accept quote requests for destinations worldwide. Route coverage, customs requirements and delivery times must be confirmed before a shipment is booked.

Can I send fragile or valuable items?

Tell us about fragile items and declare the item's value when requesting a quote. Ask our team about packaging, restricted items and insurance availability before booking.

Which items are restricted?

Review our prohibited items guidance and contact us if you are unsure whether an item can be shipped.
